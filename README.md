# Ten Seconds

A 90-second phone game about your sense of time. Part of Mind Lab: https://slowriver87.github.io/mindlabfun/

Press start, then press stop when you think 10 seconds have passed. There is no clock to watch. Three tries are quiet. Three are busy: numbers flash up and you tap odd or even while still timing 10 seconds. Quiet and busy tries alternate.

## What it shows

- **Quiet and busy averages**: how much real time passed when 10 seconds felt done.
- **Busy-time stretch**: how much longer you waited when busy. Busy time feels shorter, so most people wait longer.
- **Odd/even accuracy**: checks that you were really busy.
- **Chart** of all six tries against the 10-second mark, plus a plain-English explanation.
- **History** of your last 20 runs, saved in your browser only.

The numbers appear at uneven intervals so they can't be used as a beat to count along with.

## Limits

Six tries is a small sample, and counting in your head defeats the test.
